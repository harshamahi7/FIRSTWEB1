# Visakha Dairy - Modern Digital Transformation & E-Commerce Redesign

A redesign concept for **Sri Vijaya Visakha Milk Producers Company Limited** ([visakhadairy.com](https://www.visakhadairy.com)), transforming the traditional informational website into a **modern, premium, responsive dairy e-commerce platform with a milk subscription experience**, while preserving the authentic brand identity, cooperative history, and farmer-first heritage.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

1. **Brand Identity & Heritage**
   - Iconic Visakha Dairy green (`#006838`) & dairy ocean blue visual identity.
   - Authentic company history from 1973 (founded by Sri Adari Tulasirao) through the 1977 Akkireddipalem plant, 1999 MACS Act, 2006 Producer Company conversion, to the 2018 Rangampeta automated mega plant.
   - Board of Directors & executive leadership (Chairman Sri A. Anand Kumar, MD Sri S. V. Ramana).
   - MP & EEH Welfare Trust highlighting free rural hospitals and schools for 3.5 Lakh+ member dairy farmers.

2. **Full Product Portfolio (14 Categories)**
   - **Fresh Milk:** Toned Milk, Pure Cow Milk, Shakti Standardised Milk, Gold Full Cream, Smart Double Toned.
   - **UHT Milk:** Good Milk Brik, Slim Skimmed Milk.
   - **Curd & Yogurt:** Fresh Thick Curd (Pouches & Buckets), Bengal Mishti Doi.
   - **Chaas & Lassi:** Spiced Buttermilk (with green chilli, ginger & curry leaves), Sweet Lassi.
   - **Ghee & Butter:** Amruth Pure Cow Ghee, Amruth Lite Low-Cholesterol Ghee (NDRI tech), Special Dairy Ghee, Table Butter.
   - **Paneer:** Fresh Malai Paneer (200g, 500g, 1kg).
   - **Traditional Sweets:** Pure Doodhpeda, Kalakhand, Badam Burfi, Milk Cake, Pure Ghee Halwa.
   - **Bakery & Ice Cream:** Daily Fresh Milk Bread, Osmania Butter Biscuits, Real Milk Vanilla & Kulfi.
   - **Specialty Products:** Traditional Junnu (Colostrum pudding), Milk Shakes.

3. **Doorstep Daily Milk Subscription System**
   - Frequency options: **Daily**, **Alternate Days**, **Weekly**, or **Custom Schedule** (select specific weekdays).
   - Pack size & quantity selection with real-time monthly cost calculator.
   - 5% subscriber savings with zero doorstep delivery fees.
   - Dedicated **Subscription Dashboard** to Pause, Resume, edit quantity, or cancel anytime.

4. **Product Catalogue & Search**
   - Dedicated `/products` page with instant search, multi-faceted filtering (Category, Price Range, Pack Size, In-Stock), and sorting (Popular, Price Low-High, Price High-Low, Newest).
   - Product details modal with nutritional breakdown (Energy, Protein, Fat, Carbs, Calcium), ingredients, storage, FSSAI verification, and "Frequently Bought Together" bundles.

5. **Smooth 3-Step Checkout**
   - Step 1: Delivery Address (select saved or add new address).
   - Step 2: Slot & Date (Early morning 5:30 AM - 7:30 AM or evening slot).
   - Step 3: Payment (UPI, Card, Cash on Delivery).
   - Live visual order tracking timeline (`Confirmed` → `Preparing` → `Out for Delivery` → `Delivered`) with delivery agent details.

6. **Retailer & Parlour Locator**
   - Search by City (Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Kakinada, Rajahmundry) and neighborhood.
   - Interactive visual map with pins and outlet cards with directions and direct phone call links.

7. **Corporate & Downloads Hub**
   - Categorized downloads for Price Lists, Vendor Empanelment Forms, Franchise Applications, Tender Circulars, and Audited Annual Reports.
   - B2B vendor registration and retail franchise enquiry forms.

8. **Admin Operations Dashboard (`/admin`)**
   - Operations KPI metrics: Today's Orders, Total Orders, Revenue, Active Customers, Subscriptions, Low Stock alerts.
   - Sales analytics charts by day and product category.
   - Interactive order fulfillment status updater and cold-chain inventory alerts.

9. **Mobile-First Experience**
   - Dedicated mobile bottom navigation bar (`Home`, `Products`, `Subscribe`, `Cart`, `Account`).
   - Responsive touch gestures, fast loading, and smooth transitions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **React 18** with **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS** with bespoke typography and dairy brand palette
- **Lucide Icons**
- **Vite** bundler for sub-second hot reload and production optimization

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

```bash
# Navigate to project directory
cd visakha-dairy

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start development server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build
```

The web application runs locally at `http://localhost:5173/`.
